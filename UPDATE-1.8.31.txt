Tornado uses the existing account's Gmail App Password and the local Launcher's direct Gmail IMAP connection, rather than JEXI's remote job endpoint. No fixed 20-second wait. The password is selected from the stored profile account by the extension background. It is not sent to the BLS page, JEXI or another relay service. IMAP authentication goes to imap.gmail.com using TLS.
